How to play

A big truck is cruising down the highway with an empty trailer. Your job: get every car on board.

1

Steer

Slide your car between lanes and line it up behind the moving trailer.

2

Dodge

Weave through buses, vans and racers. One bump and it’s a cartoon crash!

3

Hitch!

Drive into the green zone behind the trailer to load your car, then do it again with the next car before the clock runs out.

Ways to drive

From London to Japan to New York, every level brings new traffic, new scenery and new ways to play.

Highway Hitch

Load every car onto the moving trailer while dodging highway traffic. Fill all the spots to win.

Tow-truck rescues

Hook up broken-down cars calling for HELP and haul them to the tow yard.

Figure-8 races

Race around a figure-8 track and time the crossing so you don’t get T-boned.

🏁

Figure-8 survival

How long can you last? Keep looping the 8 without crashing.

Power-ups!

Earn coins as you play, then grab a power-up when a level gets tough. Or watch a short video for a free one.

🛡️

Shield

Saves you from one crash.

⏳

Slow-Mo

Slows all the other cars for 6 seconds. You still zoom at full speed.

🧲

Magnet

Pulls your car straight onto the trailer, and nothing can hit it on the way.

✈️

Fly

Your car sprouts wings! Soar over the traffic and land on the trailer from any side.

🪙 Coins are earned in the game only. There’s nothing to buy.
