Paw HarborRescue

A storm jammed the harbor, and little pets are stranded. Can you clear the way?

A cozy puzzle game for kids and families. Tap the boats in the right order to untangle the harbor, rescue the pets, and give them a warm, happy home.

Tap, untangle, rescue.

Every level is a harbor full of boats pointing every which way. Find the right order and watch them sail free.
