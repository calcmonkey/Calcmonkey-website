Rescue

A storm jammed the harbor and left little pets stranded. Clear the boats to bring them home.

A cozy boat puzzle game for kids and families. Tap the boats in the right order to untangle the harbor, rescue the pets waiting on the dock, and earn coins to pamper them.

The puzzle

Tap, untangle, rescue.

Every level is a harbor packed with boats going every which way. Work out the right order and watch the jam melt away.
