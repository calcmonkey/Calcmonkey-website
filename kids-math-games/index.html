Free, fun math games for kids in grades 1–6

Kids learn math best when it feels like play. In the CalcMonkey Kids Jungle, every right answer earns a banana, and bananas unlock trips for your monkey — the beach, a birthday party, outer space and more — with new outfits to try on. Pick a grade, then choose a quick-facts game or a step-by-step puzzle. If a child gets stuck, Coach Monkey shows them how with a short lesson.

  • Quick math facts games

    Addition and subtraction facts for 1st and 2nd grade, times tables and division facts for 3rd–6th grade, in Banana Grab, Balloon Pop, Jungle Race and Feed the Monkeys.

  • Step-by-step math puzzles

    Number line hopping, tens and ones, carrying and borrowing, the area model for multiplication, long division, fractions, order of operations and two-step word problems — matched to each grade.

  • Built-in help

    Answers flash for younger kids who are stuck, and Coach Monkey teaches tricks like counting on, making ten and skip counting.

  • Free and safe

    No sign-up and nothing to download. Progress is saved on your own device.
