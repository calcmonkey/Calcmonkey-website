Try one together: 3 + 2 × 4²

  1. Exponents first: 4² = 4 × 4 = 16.
  2. Then multiply: 2 × 16 = 32.
  3. Then add: 3 + 32 = 35.
  4. So 3 + 2 × 4² = 35.

Tips for parents and teachers

  • For negatives, use a thermometer or elevator: below zero is just “going down”.
  • Read percent as “out of 100”: 25% of 80 is 25 out of every 100, so 20.
  • When a problem feels long, rewrite it after each step instead of doing it all in your head.

Questions parents ask

What math is taught in 6th grade?

Typically ratios and rates, percents, dividing fractions, multi-digit division, negative numbers, exponents and expressions, and an introduction to equations and statistics.
