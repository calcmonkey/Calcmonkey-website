Try one together: 84 ÷ 3 with long division

  1. Divide: 8 ÷ 3 = 2. Write 2 above the 8.
  2. Multiply and subtract: 2 × 3 = 6, and 8 − 6 = 2.
  3. Bring down the 4 to make 24.
  4. 24 ÷ 3 = 8. So 84 ÷ 3 = 28. Check: 28 × 3 = 84 ✓

Tips for parents and teachers

  • Shaky times tables make long division feel hard. Warm up with two minutes of a quick-fact game first.
  • Always check a division answer by multiplying back.
  • Estimate before solving: 84 ÷ 3 should be a bit less than 90 ÷ 3 = 30.

  • 4.NF.A.1 Explain why fractions are equivalent, using visual models.

Questions parents ask

Is long division taught in 4th grade?

Yes. Fourth graders usually divide numbers up to four digits by a one-digit number, often with models first and the standard steps later. Banana Split walks through each step.

Do 4th graders need to know times tables to 12?

Standards require facts through 10 × 10, but many schools and parents practice through 12. The quick-fact games here go up to 12 × 12 in 4th grade.
