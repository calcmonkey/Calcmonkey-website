Try one together: a two-step story

  1. “Mia has 4 bags with 6 bananas in each. She gives away 5 bananas. How many are left?”
  2. Plan: first find how many she has, then take away 5.
  3. Step 1: 4 × 6 = 24 bananas.
  4. Step 2: 24 − 5 = 19. Mia has 19 bananas left.

Tips for parents and teachers

  • Read it twice: once for the story, once for the numbers.
  • Ask “What is the question asking for?” before doing any math.
  • Check if the answer makes sense in the story — 190 bananas would be a red flag!

Questions parents ask

Why do kids struggle with word problems?

Often the math is fine but the planning isn’t — kids grab the numbers and guess an operation. Teaching them to name what they know and what they need first makes a big difference.

How can I help my child with word problems?

Have them retell the story in their own words, draw a quick picture, and say the plan out loud before calculating.
