Try one together: 15 − 8 by thinking addition

  1. Ask: 8 plus what makes 15?
  2. 8 + 2 = 10.
  3. 10 + 5 = 15.
  4. You added 2 + 5 = 7, so 15 − 8 = 7.

Tips for parents and teachers

  • Subtraction is the opposite of addition — “think addition” is often faster than counting back.
  • For numbers that are close together, like 12 − 9, count up from the smaller number.
  • Talk about “difference” as how far apart two numbers are.

Questions parents ask

Why is subtraction harder than addition for kids?

Counting back is harder than counting on, and kids have to think about which number comes first. Connecting each subtraction fact to an addition fact they already know makes it much easier.

How do I teach subtraction with borrowing?

Show that one ten can be traded for ten ones, then practice one column at a time. See our carrying and borrowing games for a step-by-step walk-through.
